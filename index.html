    Hajj with Khair

    A Pledge to Serve with Devotion and Dedication

    Green Air Travels & Tours Private Limited is one of Pakistan's most reputable Hajj service providers, authorized and accredited by the Ministry of Religious Affairs. Our commitment goes beyond logistics — we strive to create a spiritual environment where every pilgrim can perform their Hajj with full peace of mind and focus on their ibadah.

    01

    Pre-Departure Orientation

    Comprehensive Hajj training sessions covering rituals, duas, and health precautions. Our scholars guide pilgrims through every step of the Hajj journey before departure.

    02

    Luxury Accommodation in Makkah

    Premium hotels located within 200 meters of Masjid Al-Haram, allowing pilgrims maximum spiritual access with minimal fatigue. Air-conditioned rooms, halal meals, and 24/7 concierge.

    03

    Madinah — The City of Peace

    Comfortable stays near Masjid An-Nabawi with organized ziyarat visits to historical sites. Dedicated group transport and scholar-led guided tours of blessed locations.

    04

    Sacred Rites Assistance

    Experienced Hajj guides accompany pilgrims through all five pillars of Hajj — Tawaf, Sa'i, Arafat, Muzdalifah, and Mina — providing step-by-step spiritual guidance and physical support.

    05

    24/7 Emergency Support

    A dedicated team of multilingual staff is available around the clock to assist with any medical, logistical, or personal needs throughout the entire pilgrimage.
