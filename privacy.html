Privacy Policy

Green Air Travels & Tour Pvt. Ltd. values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your data when you use our website or travel-related services.

Introduction

Green Air Travels & Tour Pvt. Ltd. respects the privacy of all customers, visitors, and users of our website and services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and process information when you interact with our platform.

By using our website, booking services, or communicating with us, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory changes. Updated versions will become effective immediately after publication on this page.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal and non-personal information when you interact with our website or services.

  • Full name and contact details
  • Email address and phone number
  • Passport and identification details
  • Travel preferences and booking history
  • Billing and payment-related information
  • Technical information such as browser and device type

This information helps us improve customer experience, process bookings, and provide better support.

How We Use Information

Green Air Travels & Tour Pvt. Ltd. uses customer information only for legitimate business purposes and travel-related services.

  • Processing bookings and reservations
  • Providing customer support and communication
  • Sending travel updates and confirmations
  • Improving website functionality and user experience
  • Managing payments and billing processes
  • Preventing fraudulent or unauthorized activities

Cookies Policy

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve browsing experience and analyze website performance.

  • Cookies help remember user preferences

  • Users can disable cookies through browser settings

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to unauthorized third parties. However, information may be shared when necessary for travel arrangements and legal obligations.

  • Airlines and hotel providers

  • Payment gateway providers
  • Government and regulatory authorities if required by law

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Although we strive to protect personal data, no online system can guarantee complete security.

Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify or update this Privacy Policy at any time. Users are encouraged to review this page regularly for the latest information regarding privacy practices.
